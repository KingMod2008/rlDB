KingMod 2026.10.24.0006 — Inventory and cosmetic improvements

- Added a clear preparation banner with an animated spinner and elapsed timer. Wait for setup to finish before launching Rocket League.
- Improved automatic delivery of queued items and recovery after connection interruptions.
- Improved item loading when cars are rebuilt during a match.
- Improved shared cosmetic synchronization, including title data and equipped-item priority.
- Cosmetic synchronization starts in the lobby and supports receiving players who have not spawned items themselves.
- Added separate custom-title definitions for each player to prevent style conflicts.
- Added Party Connect: send party invites and requests to join using Epic account IDs, Steam ID64, or known Epic usernames.
- Added saved players and automatic saving of Party Connect settings.
- Rebuilt Keyboard & Mouse with a classic five-row keyboard, mouse-button display, animated presses, themes, size and position controls.
- Fixed setup getting stuck after a Rocket League executable update. KingMod preserves previous recovery files and rebuilds support for verified current packages.
- Improved recovery after verifying or updating Rocket League through Epic Games or Steam.

For shared cosmetics, both players need the latest KingMod version and a compatible sync service. Spawn and equip your custom title again after updating. Party invites and join requests require the other player's acceptance.
